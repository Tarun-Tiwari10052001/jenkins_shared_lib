## notifySlack

Global step (callable directly as `notifySlack(...)`) wrapping the Slack Notification plugin.

**Parameters** (Map):
- `color` — `good` | `warning` | `danger` (default `warning`)
- `message` — text to post (default generic notification)
- `channel` — Slack channel (default `#ci-cd-alerts`)

Requires a `slack-webhook` Jenkins credential and the workspace/team configured globally.
